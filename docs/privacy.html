Itihasa

Privacy Policy

App: Itihasa (com.nr1845.itihasa) · Developer: NR1845 · Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short. Itihasa is an offline reader of Indian mythology stories. Your profile, bookmarks and reading progress stay on your phone. The app sends only anonymous usage statistics (such as which stories are opened) to Google Firebase Analytics. It does not send your name, email, password or what you search for, and it shows no advertisements. You can switch the statistics off in Profile → Privacy.

1. Information that stays on your device

To save your place, Itihasa asks you to create a profile on your device. This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • your username and email address;
  • your password, kept only in a scrambled (salted and hashed) form, so it cannot be read back or recovered;
  • your bookmarked stories, reading progress and language choice.

Because there is no server, we cannot see or restore this information. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or using Profile → Delete profile removes it.

2. Information sent to us: anonymous usage statistics

Itihasa uses Google Analytics for Firebase (provided by Google LLC) to understand how the app is used, so we can improve it. When you use the app, it sends these events:

EventWhat it tells us
app_openThe app was opened (with the app version)
scripture_openedWhich scripture was opened (for example “ramayana”)
story_opened / story_completedWhich story was opened or read to the end (an identifier such as “ramayana-007”)
language_selectedEnglish or Hindi
story_bookmarkedWhich story was bookmarked
search_performedThat a search happened (never the words you typed)
reading_sessionHow many seconds a reading session lasted
share_clickedWhich story you chose to share

Firebase also collects some technical information automatically: a random app-instance identifier (not linked to your name, email or Google account), the device model, operating system version, app version, language, and an approximate location (country or city level) derived from your IP address. Your full IP address is not stored in Analytics reports.

Events are saved on the device when you are offline and are uploaded when a connection is available. All data is sent over an encrypted (TLS) connection.

3. What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, password, phone number, contacts, photos or files.
  • No precise (GPS) location.
  • No advertising ID. The app is configured not to collect it, and it does not request the advertising permission.
  • No advertisements are shown, and no personalised advertising is used. If advertising is ever added, this policy will be updated first and you will be informed.

4. How we use the information

Only to understand which stories and features people use (for example popular stories, language preference, how long people read and whether they return) and to fix and improve the app. We do not sell your information and do not use it to profile you.

5. Sharing

Usage statistics are processed by Google as our service provider under the Google Analytics and Firebase terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. We do not share your information with anyone else, except if required by law.

6. How long we keep it

Usage statistics are not linked to your identity. We keep user-level event data for no longer than 14 months, the maximum period Google Analytics offers for it; summarised reports (counts and totals) may be kept longer. Profile data on your device is kept until you delete it.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • Switch off statistics: Profile → Privacy → turn off “Share anonymous usage statistics”. Nothing is sent while it is off.
  • Delete your profile and data: Profile → Delete profile. This removes your profile, bookmarks and progress from the device and resets the analytics identifier, so earlier statistics can no longer be connected to this installation. Uninstalling the app has a similar effect.
  • Questions or requests: email nayan.rathi1845@gmail.com. Because statistics are anonymous, we usually cannot find data about one individual; the steps above, and the retention limit, are how it is removed. See also how to delete your data.

8. Children

Itihasa is intended for teenagers and adults (13 and over) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Data sent to Firebase travels over TLS. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we collect as little as we can.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date, and in-app notice will be given for material changes.
