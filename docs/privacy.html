Itihasa

Privacy Policy

App: Itihasa (com.nr1845.itihasa) · Developer: NR1845 · Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short. Itihasa is a reader of Indian mythology stories. To sign in, your email address, username and password are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. Your bookmarks and reading progress stay on your phone. Story pictures are downloaded from our image server. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics (such as which stories are opened) to Google Firebase Analytics. It shows no advertisements. You can switch the statistics off in Profile → Privacy, and delete your account in Profile → Delete account.

1. Your account, and what stays on your device

Itihasa asks you to create an account. Accounts are provided by Firebase Authentication (Google LLC), which stores:

  • your email address;
  • your username (display name);
  • your password, which Google keeps only in a protected (hashed) form. We cannot see it;
  • technical sign-in details such as account creation and last sign-in time, and the IP address used when signing in.

We use this only to let you sign in, reset a forgotten password and delete your account.

The following is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone and is not sent to us: your bookmarked stories, reading progress and language choice, and the story pictures you have already viewed. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them from the phone; your account itself remains until you delete it.

Story pictures. Illustrations are downloaded from our image server (Firebase Hosting, Google LLC) the first time a story is opened, then kept on your phone. As with any web download, the server receives your IP address and the name of the picture requested; these requests are not linked to your account.

2. Anonymous usage statistics

Itihasa uses Google Analytics for Firebase (provided by Google LLC) to understand how the app is used, so we can improve it. When you use the app, it sends these events:

EventWhat it tells us
app_openThe app was opened (with the app version)
scripture_openedWhich scripture was opened (for example “ramayana”)
story_opened / story_completedWhich story was opened or read to the end (an identifier such as “ramayana-007”)
language_selectedEnglish or Hindi
story_bookmarkedWhich story was bookmarked
search_performedThat a search happened (never the words you typed)
reading_sessionHow many seconds a reading session lasted
share_clickedWhich story you chose to share

Firebase also collects some technical information automatically: a random app-instance identifier (not linked to your name, email or Google account), the device model, operating system version, app version, language, and an approximate location (country or city level) derived from your IP address. Your full IP address is not stored in Analytics reports.

Events are saved on the device when you are offline and are uploaded when a connection is available. All data is sent over an encrypted (TLS) connection.

3. What we do not collect

  • No phone number, contacts, photos or files. Your email address and username are used only for your account (section 1) and are never sent to Analytics.
  • No precise (GPS) location.
  • No advertising ID. The app is configured not to collect it, and it does not request the advertising permission.
  • No advertisements are shown, and no personalised advertising is used. If advertising is ever added, this policy will be updated first and you will be informed.

4. How we use the information

Only to understand which stories and features people use (for example popular stories, language preference, how long people read and whether they return) and to fix and improve the app. We do not sell your information and do not use it to profile you.

5. Sharing

Account details, picture downloads and usage statistics are processed by Google as our service provider under the Google Analytics and Firebase terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. We do not share your information with anyone else, except if required by law.

6. How long we keep it

Usage statistics are not linked to your identity. We keep user-level event data for no longer than 14 months, the maximum period Google Analytics offers for it; summarised reports (counts and totals) may be kept longer. Your account is kept until you delete it; Google removes deleted account data from its systems within its standard deletion period (up to about 180 days). Data on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • Switch off statistics: Profile → Privacy → turn off “Share anonymous usage statistics”. Nothing is sent while it is off.
  • Delete your account and data: Profile → Delete account, then enter your password. This permanently deletes your account from Firebase Authentication, removes your bookmarks and progress from the device and resets the analytics identifier. If you cannot use the app, email us from the account's email address and we will delete the account for you.
  • Questions or requests: email nayan.rathi1845@gmail.com. Usage statistics are anonymous, so we usually cannot find statistics about one individual; the retention limit is how they are removed. See also how to delete your data.

8. Children

Itihasa is intended for teenagers and adults (13 and over) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Security

Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never stored on your phone or visible to us. All data travels over an encrypted (TLS) connection; the app refuses unencrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we collect as little as we can.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date, and in-app notice will be given for material changes.
