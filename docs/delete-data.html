Delete your data

App: Itihasa (com.nr1845.itihasa) · Developer: NR1845

You can delete your Itihasa account and its data yourself, inside the app:

  1. Open Itihasa and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Permanently delete my account.

What is deleted: your account (email address, username and password) is permanently deleted from Google Firebase Authentication; your bookmarks and reading progress are removed from the phone; the anonymous analytics identifier is reset.

If you cannot use the app (for example you uninstalled it or forgot your password), email nayan.rathi1845@gmail.com from the email address of your account and ask for it to be deleted. We will delete it within 30 days.

What is kept, and for how long: Google may keep deleted account data in its backup systems for up to about 180 days before it is fully removed. Bookmarks and progress exist only on your phone; uninstalling the app removes them.

To stop usage statistics without deleting anything, turn off Share anonymous usage statistics under Profile → Privacy.

Anonymous usage statistics already sent to Google Firebase Analytics are not linked to your name or email and are automatically deleted after at most 14 months. For any other question, email nayan.rathi1845@gmail.com.
