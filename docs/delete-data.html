Delete your data

App: Itihasa (com.itihasa.app) · Developer: NR1845

Itihasa keeps your profile, bookmarks and reading progress only on your phone. We do not hold a copy on any server, so you can remove everything yourself:

  1. Open Itihasa and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete profile and confirm.

This deletes your profile, bookmarks and reading progress from the device and resets the anonymous analytics identifier. If you no longer have the app, uninstalling it removes the data stored on your phone.

To stop usage statistics without deleting anything, turn off Share anonymous usage statistics under Profile → Privacy.

Anonymous usage statistics already sent to Google Firebase Analytics are not linked to your name or email and are automatically deleted after at most 14 months. If you have a question or a request, email nayan.rathi1845@gmail.com.
